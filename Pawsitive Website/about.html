About Pawsitive Pet Academy

Educating pet owners and future pet-care professionals.

OUR STORY

Transforming Durban's Pet Dynamics

Pawsitive Pet Academy was established in 2023 in Durban by Sarah Smith. We aim to educate pet owners and future pet-care professionals about responsible animal care.

We offer six-month professional programmes and six-week practical skills courses to help learners develop their knowledge and confidence.

Our goal is to create a learning environment where people can gain practical experience and understand the needs of animals.
