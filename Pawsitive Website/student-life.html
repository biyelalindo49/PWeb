Student Life

Learn, practise and grow your passion for animal care.

Welcome to Student Life

At Pawsitive Pet Academy, student life is about more than learning from books. Students develop knowledge, practical skills and an understanding of how to care for animals.

Our learning environment encourages responsibility, patience, teamwork and kindness towards pets.

Through our pet-care programmes and practical courses, students can build confidence and develop skills that may help them pursue opportunities in the pet-care industry.

What Students Experience

Our learning approach combines knowledge with practical activities.

Classroom Learning

Students learn about animal behaviour, responsible pet ownership, health and general animal welfare.

Practical Activities

Practical learning helps students understand pet-care routines and apply their knowledge.

Teamwork

Students are encouraged to share ideas, support one another and develop communication skills.

Skills for the Future

We encourage students to develop patience, confidence, responsibility and compassion. These qualities are important when working with animals and their owners.
