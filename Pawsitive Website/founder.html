OUR FOUNDER

Sarah Smith

Founder of Pawsitive Pet Academy

Sarah Smith established Pawsitive Pet Academy in Durban in 2023 with a vision of helping people understand animals and provide better pet care.

She believes that education and practical experience can help pet owners become more confident and help future professionals develop valuable skills.

Through Pawsitive Pet Academy, her goal is to create a welcoming learning environment where compassion, responsible care and quality education are encouraged.
