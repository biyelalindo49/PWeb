COURSE DETAILS

Canine Obedience Training

Build a stronger relationship with your dog through positive training and practical learning.

ABOUT THIS COURSE

Understand Your Dog Better

Our Canine Obedience Training course introduces learners to positive training methods that encourage good behaviour and communication.

Learners explore basic commands, dog body language, reward-based training and responsible handling techniques.

The course is suitable for people who want to improve their understanding of dogs and develop practical training skills.

COURSE INFORMATION

What You Will Learn

Dog Behaviour

Understand common body-language signals and basic canine behaviour.

Basic Commands

Explore commands such as sit, stay and recall.

Positive Training

Learn how rewards, consistency and patience support training.

Responsible Care

Develop an understanding of safe handling and animal wellbeing.
